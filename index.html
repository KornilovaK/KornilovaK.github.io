RESEARCH & EXPERIMENTS

Testing ideas.
Learning from results.

I evaluate approaches against the task, the evidence and the resources they require.

Question the data

Investigate label quality and changing definitions before relying on additional training data or aggregate metrics.

Check the evaluation

Look for leakage, changing conditions and weak baselines before drawing conclusions from offline experiments.

Choose a suitable method

Compare simpler statistical methods, trained models and LLMs, and investigate failure cases before choosing a solution.

HOW I BUILD ✳

From experiments
to evidence.

Reliable systems start with clear evaluation and an honest look at where models fail.

Independent ownership & collaboration

In my current role, I independently lead projects, explore alternative approaches and test hypotheses to find the best solution. I work closely with business managers, backend and frontend developers, DevOps and other specialists to bring that work into products.

From problem to production

I define the problem and evaluation with stakeholders, explore and test candidate approaches, and prepare the chosen solution for integration. I work with domain experts on labeling and success criteria, and with engineering teams on deployment and monitoring.

Model training & post-training

Fine-tuned transformer models for classification, retrieval and generation, including task-specific classifiers, embedding models and rerankers. Worked on model distillation using supervised fine-tuning (SFT) and direct preference optimization (DPO). Experienced in multi-GPU training with Hugging Face Accelerate.

Retrieval & ranking

Trained domain-specific embedding models and rerankers, and combined dense retrieval with reranking for search and RAG. Researched non-personalized recommendation approaches using time-decayed popularity and Bayesian smoothing.

Evaluation & data

Built multi-stage LLM labeling pipelines and calibrated evaluation against domain-expert labels, investigating disagreement patterns and inconsistent reference examples.

Speech recognition

Worked on voice input and compared speech recognition models, including an offline alternative. Evaluated recognition errors in numbers, abbreviations and terminology, combining quantitative metrics with qualitative review.

A BIT ABOUT ME ✳

Curious by nature.
Practical by design.

I’m interested in using my ML experience in areas such as healthcare, environmental protection and humanitarian work, particularly on tools that support specialists in their work.

I believe ML is most useful when the problem and its intended impact come first. I look for solutions that use resources thoughtfully and fit the task, whether that means a trained model, a simpler statistical method or an LLM. I want to help shape both the outcome we are working toward and the methods we choose.

I enjoy the full journey from framing a problem and designing an evaluation to building a service people can use. In my current role, I lead projects independently, test hypotheses and collaborate with people across disciplines.
